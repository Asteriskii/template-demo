02Three questions the reporting raises

Three directions, one question we cannot answer

2 core journeys

Forecast Calculation (132 users) and Data Explorer (112) carry the most traffic, and in both the thing people do most is the same: read a report.

The thing people do most — how do we make it smoother?

141 access prompts

Seen in September. Of 102 affected visits, 59.8% had no later work step recorded — 58 users.

Behind those 141, how many people got access and came back to finish?

1 in 7 did not return

Repeat rates of 86.4% and 84.1% mean roughly one in seven users never came back.

Is the task simply infrequent, or did that one attempt not work out?

All three point to the same set of answers: did the task get finished, what stopped it, and how much effort it took.

03What we propose to measure

Three directions, each closing one gap

Data Explorer · Forecast Calculation

The report-viewing experience

Turn "how many times it was opened" into "how many people, and did they get the number".

  • How many people hit a snag
  • How many of them were blocked
  • How they dealt with it
Access Management

From access request to finished work

Link blocked, guided, requested, approved and returned into one traceable line.

  • Who was blocked, and what access
  • Did they request the right one
  • How many returned, and after how long
General

Silent failure

Record the step people stop at, making a failure that leaves no trace visible for the first time.

  • Share unfinished with no error
  • Which step people stop at
  • First-time vs repeat gap

The three directions share one set of core numbers, so this is one piece of work, not three.

05How we start

Four steps to the first improvement list

  1. Agree what a finished task means

    One working session to fix the start point, the finished signal and the failure rule for both journeys, and confirm the numbers each direction needs.

    UX + Forecast pod

  2. Confirm what existing data already covers

    Check with engineering and data, list what is reusable and what is missing, and get an effort estimate.

    Engineering + data

  3. Run Calculation and Data Explorer end to end

    From the user's action all the way to a number on the report, verified by QA and data before anything scales.

    Engineering + QA

  4. Produce the first improvement list

    With the first real numbers in hand, rank by people affected multiplied by severity, and take it straight into the next planning round.

    UX + PO
