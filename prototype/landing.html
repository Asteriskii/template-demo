Why templates
For Engineering

Less duplicate code

Skip rebuilding proven patterns from scratch — spend the saved time on what's actually unique.

For Business

Faster requirement discussions

Everyone points at the same reference, so there's less back-and-forth over who understood what differently.

For Users

Lower learning curve

Similar scenarios look alike — learn one, and you've basically learned the whole category.

No matter the request

It has to hold up against these

This isn't extra process — it's our baseline for our own work. Everything gets checked against these five before it ships, from a single button to a full page.

Component Library Reuse what exists instead of reinventing it every time.
Design Patterns Follow the interaction patterns the team has already settled on, so users don't have to relearn anything.
Accessibility Keyboard, screen readers, color contrast — make sure everyone can use it.
Customer Thinking Understand the user's problem first, then decide how to solve it.
Quality Hold up to scrutiny before shipping — not just "it works."
The UX team is consolidating similar user journeys into reusable templates — this site is where each template's status, design, and rollout become visible.
